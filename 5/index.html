Observations:

  • Generated outputs resemble blurry digit-like shapes
  • Digits appear averaged, ambiguous, or incomplete
  • No clear separation between digits 0–9
  • Outputs look similar across samples

Reasoning: When trained with MSE to denoise pure noise, the UNet learns to output the average of the training images, resulting in blurry digit-like structures that reflect the centroid of the MNIST dataset rather than distinct samples.
